I own two cameras and I haven't been able to choose just one. Each has a specific role.

The **Sony A7 Mark II** is my camera for important occasions. Full frame, heavy, technically superior. I use it for planned trips, scheduled shoots, situations where I want the highest quality. It's a professional tool that delivers solid, reliable image quality.

The **Fuji X-T30** is compact and light, with a vintage design I appreciate. It travels everywhere with me — in my everyday bag, in a backpack, wherever I go. Fuji's film simulation is one of its strengths: it produces ready-to-use shots without needing post-production.

Two different approaches. Sony focuses on technical power, Fuji on the shooting experience. Full frame for maximum quality, APS-C for portability.

I keep both, because depending on the context and needs, one is more suitable than the other.
