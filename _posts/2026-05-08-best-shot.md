That evening at Golden Gate Overlook, I wasn't looking for anything special. Just wanted to enjoy the sunset, maybe snap a few routine shots. Then it happened.

The fog was rising from the bay, slow but relentless, like it wanted to eat the bridge piece by piece. And in that instant I witnessed the meeting of absurd colors. Orange, gold, a red I didn't even know existed. And there, right in the middle of all that chaos of light and mist, a small flock of birds decided to cross the frame at exactly the right moment.

Sometimes your best shot isn't one you plan. It's one you meet.
